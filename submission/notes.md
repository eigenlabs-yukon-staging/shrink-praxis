# Degree-24 cyclotomic packing on the 4,446-form grid

The coefficient ring uses the order-39 cyclotomic polynomial. Fast exact
evaluation and interpolation preserve uniform seed entries and the coefficient
infinity-norm SIS reduction. The 185 ring rows give 4,440 unrolled rows, with
six padded form positions. The protected gate computes 5,561 MiB and 22,513
million field multiplications.
