# Praxis with alpha-scaled sparse quotient powers

The sparse quotient powers whose exponents are divisible by the coset size supply the alpha factor in G1. Their G2 quotient bases and the associated non-key bases are shared across the two decryption computations. The other alpha factors come from G2. All three original stack ties are retained.

The exact gate passes all 18 rules at 7,938 MiB and 26,426 million field multiplications.
