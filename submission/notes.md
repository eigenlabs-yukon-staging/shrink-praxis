# Three-tag Praxis with factored exponent evaluation

The construction keeps the public mixed correction tuples and evaluates their exponents using the sparse coset polynomial. A coset product tree computes QAP cofactors, and the right-leg coordinates share products across legs. The smaller scalar ledger permits full degree-16 packing at 4,356 forms.

The protected gate reports 5,500 MiB and 29,319 million field multiplications.
