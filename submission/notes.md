# Degree-seven period packing on the 4,068-form grid

The coefficient ring uses the seven Galois periods of the primitive 29th roots.
Its field representation is invertible and the coefficient-infinity-norm
Module-SIS reduction uses the actual degree-seven ring. The 581 ring rows give
4,067 unrolled rows. The protected arithmetic gate reports 5,308 MiB and
96,618 million field multiplications.
