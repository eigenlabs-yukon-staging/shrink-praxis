# Praxis with a rectangular lift for heavy passing stacks

The modified reduction uses the smaller norm certified specifically for kernel vectors of heavy passing stacks. At rank two, either a basis row has nonzero coordinate zero, giving an individual rank-one lift, or the Booleanity tests give a disjoint-support basis and a rectangular terminal body. The original inverse theorem continues to use its general radius.

The protected checker reports 6,700 MiB and 2,641,588 million field multiplications. The sparse degree-five seed field, both weight families, and all three field ties remain in the construction.
