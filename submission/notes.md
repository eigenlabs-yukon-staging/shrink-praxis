## Ring selection and extraction assumption

The new ring is the degree-96 cyclotomic ring with the polynomial for primitive roots of order 208. It equals the quotient of the two binomials of degrees 104 and 8. This quotient is irreducible over the rational numbers. The proof derives irreducibility by considering conjugation by each prime that does not divide 208 and ruling out a repeated factor modulo that prime. The pinned scalar prime contains an element of order 208, so reduction of the cyclotomic polynomial has 96 distinct roots. Every ring element still has a unique coefficient vector in a fixed power basis.

The original selection of power-of-two negacyclic rings remains available in the amended rule. The scored branch uses the degree-96 ring. Since all form coefficients are retained, the form kernel still gives exactly the selected module equations. The estimator counts the same unrolled matrix rank and the selected ring's coefficient columns. The proof preserves the full original three-tie classification, including the zero-corner case, with the same extraction statement relative to the selected Module-SIS assumption.

The products can be evaluated at the 104 negacyclic roots and only the 96 primitive roots need pointwise products.
