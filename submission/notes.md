# Three-tag Praxis with paired matrix products

This configuration uses the protected degree-sixteen ring and packs fourteen ordinary coordinates per column. Paired scalar products share each column correction across two mask evaluations. Prefix and suffix products share the QAP cofactors, and equal right-leg types reuse their scalar factors. The unified search bound covers both ordinary and evaluation-point logarithms.

The protected gate reports 5536 MiB and 27153 million field multiplications.
