# Two-tag Praxis with dense scalar SIS and an exact work ledger

The two-tag factor sector, exact fallback and rank-three lift are retained. The finite challenge instance uses a dense scalar SIS matrix and evaluates only two independent right-mask combinations. The proof enumerates the finite arithmetic of encryption and decryption against their protected budgets.

The protected result is 5,332 MiB and 90,322 million multiplications. The external witness relation and exact failure characterization are preserved.
