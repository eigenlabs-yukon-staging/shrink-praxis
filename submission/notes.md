# Praxis with fully shared quotient combinations

The construction uses the 4,524-form degree-32 shape and a 174 × 13 grid, with four quotient chunks and four remainder chunks. It shares all quotient G1 combinations and publishes one extra residue base per non-key cell in each G2 sector.

The protected numerical gate passes all 18 amended rules at 7,956 MiB and 26,478 million base-field multiplications. The proof accounts for the changed publication, all group counts, correctness and extraction.
