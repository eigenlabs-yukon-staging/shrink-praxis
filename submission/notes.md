# Three-tag Praxis with public mixed corrections

Decryption derives the target correction from three witness-dependent quotient tuples and two public mixed-tag tuples. The ciphertext includes the mixed tuples and the ordinary sparse and chunked powers. The same right correction bases serve all four corners.

The protected arithmetic gate reports 5,476 MiB and 31,532 million field multiplications. The amended construction and its finite costs are proved in `proof.md`.
