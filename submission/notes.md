# Praxis with independent extension padding and sharper inverse bounds

The candidate uses ring degree 32, 141 Module-SIS rows, 12 leading form slots and 4,524 forms. The grid is 174 × 13, with 10 quotient chunks and 11 remainder chunks.

The numerical gate passes all 18 amended rules. It reports 7,931 MiB and 28,299 million base-field multiplications, compared with the paper's 8,073 MiB and 28,669 million. The pinned hardness estimate is 103.533 in base-2 log units at block size 242. The proof gives the new permitted extension padding, rank-two geometric bound and rank-three short-vector bound.
